Human population fitting data and source
========================================

Files
-----
human_population_fits_publication.py
    Python script used for the generalized geometrical / generalized T-Z,
    original c=1 T-Z, power-law, and exponential fits to selected human
    population regimes.

human_population_appendix_data.csv
    Machine-readable transcription of the population data in the appendix
    table of Sojecka and Drozd-Rzoska (2024). Population is in millions.
    Negative years denote BCE years in the transcription.

Sojecka_Drozd-Rzoska_appendix_table.pdf
    Appendix table supplied with the source material and used to construct
    the CSV file.

Primary source to cite
----------------------
A. A. Sojecka and A. Drozd-Rzoska,
"Global population: from Super-Malthus behavior to Doomsday criticality,"
Scientific Reports 14, 9853 (2024).
DOI: 10.1038/s41598-024-60589-3
Published 29 April 2024.

The human-population analysis in Zaccone and Trachenko (2026) explicitly
states that its empirical population data are taken from this reference,
including its appendix table.
